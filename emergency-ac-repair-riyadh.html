Is Your AC Emergency on This List?

If your AC shows any of these symptoms — call us now. Every minute in Riyadh summer heat matters.

🌡️
AC Not Cooling — مكيف لا يبرد
Running but blowing warm air. Usually refrigerant (Freon) leak or blocked coil. Fixed same visit in 90% of cases.
💧
AC Leaking Water — مكيف يقطر مياه
Water dripping inside your room. Blocked drain pipe or frozen evaporator. Causes wall damage if left. Fix immediately.
⚡
AC Suddenly Stopped — مكيف توقف فجأة
Complete shutdown. Capacitor failure, tripped breaker, or PCB fault. One of our most common emergency calls in Riyadh.
🔊
Strange Loud Noise — صوت غريب من المكيف
Banging, grinding, or rattling. Fan motor or loose compressor parts. If left running, risk of total compressor failure.
🔥
Burning Smell — رائحة حرق
Electrical fault — TURN THE AC OFF IMMEDIATELY and call us. Do not run it. Fire risk. We respond in under 2 hours.
📱
Error Code Showing — كود خطأ على الشاشة
E1, E3, F5, P1 and others — our technicians carry diagnostic tools for all brands and clear codes on the first visit.
🔄
AC Turning On/Off — مكيف يشتغل وينطفئ
Short cycling — runs for 2-3 minutes then shuts off. Overheating, capacitor, or refrigerant issue. Urgent repair needed.
❄️
Ice on AC Unit — تجمد على المكيف
Ice forming on indoor or outdoor unit. Blocked airflow or low refrigerant. Turn off immediately and call us.
