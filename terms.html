Our services

Hafiz Air Conditioning provides AC repair, AC cleaning, gas refill, installation, washing machine repair, refrigerator repair, and microwave repair services in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia. All work is quoted and agreed with the customer before it begins, following a free in-home diagnosis.

Pricing and payment

Prices shown on this website are starting prices and may vary based on the specific fault, parts required, and appliance brand. You will always receive a written quote after the free diagnosis and before any repair work begins.

Warranty

Repairs carried out by Hafiz Air Conditioning are covered by a 2-year warranty on parts and labor for the specific fault repaired. This warranty does not cover unrelated faults, misuse, or damage occurring after the repair.

Booking and cancellation

Bookings can be made by phone, WhatsApp, or through the website form. If you need to cancel or reschedule, please contact us as early as possible so we can offer the slot to another customer.
