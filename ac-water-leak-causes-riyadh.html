Why Is Your AC Leaking Water? – لماذا يسرب أو ينقط المكيف ماءً؟ الأسباب والحلول

A puddle under your AC looks worse than it usually is. Here's how to know which situation you're in.

You Walk In and There's a Puddle. Now What? – دخلت الغرفة ووجدت بركة ماء. ماذا الآن؟

Water pooling under your AC is one of those problems that looks worse than it usually is — and sometimes it's exactly as bad as it looks. The trick is knowing which situation you're in.

Why This Happens – لماذا يحدث هذا؟

Your AC pulls moisture out of the air. That water travels out through a small drain pipe. When something blocks that path, the water has nowhere to go but into your room.

Blocked Drain Line (Most Common) – انسداد أنبوب التصريف (الأكثر شيوعاً)

Dust and debris build up inside the drain pipe over months. In Riyadh, dust is basically a permanent guest in every AC, so this happens faster here than elsewhere.

Dirty Air Filter – فلتر الهواء المتسخ

A clogged filter restricts airflow over the coil. The coil gets too cold, ice forms, and when it melts, it melts faster than the drain pan can handle.

Wrong Installation Angle – زاوية التركيب الخاطئة

Split AC indoor units need a slight tilt toward the drain outlet. If installed level or angled wrong, water pools inside instead of draining out.

Low Refrigerant – نقص غاز التبريد

Less refrigerant means lower pressure, which can freeze the coil — same result as a dirty filter.

What You Can Check Yourself – ما يمكنك فحصه بنفسك

  • Turn off the unit if the leak is inside and getting worse – أطفئ الوحدة إذا كان التسرب بالداخل ويزداد سوءاً
  • Check and clean the filter — this alone fixes many leak complaints – افحص ونظّف الفلتر — هذا وحده يحل الكثير من الشكاوى
  • Look at the outdoor drain pipe for blockage (yes, even bird's nests happen) – افحص أنبوب التصريف الخارجي بحثاً عن انسداد
  • Try Dry mode for a few hours if humidity seems the cause – جرّب وضع Dry لبضع ساعات إذا كانت الرطوبة هي السبب

When You Need a Technician – متى تحتاج فنياً

If the leak keeps returning after cleaning the filter, or the unit isn't cooling properly alongside the leak, that's your sign. A blocked drain line usually needs proper vacuum-suction clearing — and low refrigerant needs a professional gas refill, not a DIY fix.

Bottom Line – الخلاصة

Most AC water leaks in Riyadh trace back to dust — in the filter, the drain line, or both. Check those two things first, before it turns into a bigger repair.

FAQ

Common Questions

Can I fix an AC water leak myself? – هل يمكنني إصلاح تسرب ماء المكيف بنفسي؟
Cleaning the filter and checking the outdoor drain pipe for blockage often solves it. If it persists, the drain line needs professional vacuum clearing.
Is a water leak from the AC dangerous? – هل تسرب الماء من المكيف خطير؟
It's not dangerous immediately, but left alone it can damage ceiling paint, flooring, and even the AC's own electronics over time.
