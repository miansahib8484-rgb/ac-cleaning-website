Who We Are · من نحن في الرياض

From One Van to a Certified AC Company

من سيارة واحدة إلى أكثر شركة مكيفات موثوقة في الرياض

Hafiz Air Conditioning was founded in Riyadh in 2006 by two HVAC engineers who found that the AC service market lacked honesty, quality, and accountability. They decided to change that.

Starting with a single service van and two technicians, and one simple promise — honest work, transparent pricing, and full accountability — we built our reputation across Riyadh customer by customer, job by job.

Today we operate 20+ fully equipped service vans, a team of 10+ HVAC-certified engineers and electronics technicians, and a 24/7 emergency dispatch centre — covering all Riyadh districts from Al Quds to North Riyadh.

Hafiz AC Repairing Service operates under the same Hafiz Air Conditioning brand and team, providing the identical certified technicians, pricing, and 2-year warranty across every job in Riyadh.

Which areas of Riyadh does Hafiz Air Conditioning service?

Why should I trust Hafiz Air Conditioning over another company?
We've served Riyadh since 2006 with factory-trained technicians, transparent written quotes before any work, genuine OEM parts, and a 2-year warranty on every repair. Our 4.8★ Google rating reflects 500+ satisfied customers.
Do you repair central and ducted AC units, or only split AC?
We service all AC types in Riyadh — split AC, central AC, ducted/dolabi systems, and window units. Our technicians are factory-trained on Daikin, Mitsubishi, Samsung, LG, Carrier, and Gree.

Are your technicians certified and insured?
Yes. All Hafiz Air Conditioning technicians are factory-trained, background-checked, and the company is fully licensed and insured to operate in Riyadh.
Do you offer service on Fridays, weekends, or holidays?
Yes — we operate 7 days a week including Fridays, weekends, and Saudi public holidays, with 24/7 emergency response available.
